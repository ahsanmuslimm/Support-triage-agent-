# S9.6: SLA Engine

Temporal durable timers, business hours calculation, and breach prediction.

## Implementation Summary
- SLAPolicy: Define P0-P3 targets (P0 15min, P1 1h, P2 4h, P3 24h response) + resolution times
- BusinessCalendar: Tenant-configured hours, timezone, holidays, DST handling
- SLATimer: Durable timers with pause/resume, warning at 75% deadline
- Temporal deadline: created_at + business_hours_elapsed(policy.resolution_minutes)
- Events: escalation_warning (75%), escalation_breach (100%)
- SLA query: Get timer status, time remaining, breach prediction
- Policy matching: ticket properties (priority, category, segment) → policy
- ~400 lines code + 35 tests

## Key Classes
- SLAPolicy: Policy definition with timeouts
- SLATimer: Timer state tracking with pause/resume
- SLAEngine: Policy matching and timer management
- BusinessCalendar: Business hours with holiday support
- SLAEvent: Event log entries
- SLAEventType: Enum of event types
